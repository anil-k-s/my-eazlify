Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Eazlify ("we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used and handled when you visit our website or contact us about consulting and professional services.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit the contact form on this website, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The subject of your enquiry, if provided
  • The message you submit

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, health information, or confidential business or client information through the contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your questions and enquiries.
  • Discuss potential consulting engagements or projects.
  • Follow up on your request where appropriate.
  • Prevent spam, abuse and misuse of the contact form.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not intend to use contact-form information for unrelated marketing purposes.

3. Website Hosting and Third-Party Services

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Contact-form submissions are processed through Web3Forms, which forwards submitted enquiries to our designated email inbox.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services. Depending on their infrastructure, information may be processed in countries other than the country in which you reside.

For further information, please review their policies:

  • Web3Forms Privacy Policy
  • GitHub General Privacy Statement

4. Data Retention

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, manage prospective professional relationships, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Web3Forms may retain submitted information according to its own policies and the retention settings available for our account. We may also retain enquiry emails in our inbox. We will take reasonable steps to delete information that is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell contact-form information. We may share information with service providers involved in operating the website or delivering submissions, or where disclosure is required by applicable law.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through this website. However, no internet transmission, website, or email service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to raise a concern about how it is handled.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address below. We will respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for professional enquiries and is not directed at children. Please do not submit children's personal information through this website.

9. International Visitors

Visitors in different countries may have additional rights under local privacy laws. We will handle personal information in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal obligations change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
